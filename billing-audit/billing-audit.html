How recovery is estimated: The tool tries to learn the “correct telehealth paid amount” per service_code from rows that appear correctly billed (POS 10 or POS 02 with modifier 95). For flagged rows, recovery is estimated as max(0, expected_telehealth_paid − insurance_paid). If your upload has no correctly billed examples for a code, it falls back to a default loss estimate ($35–$60 range) derived from the billing rules reference.
