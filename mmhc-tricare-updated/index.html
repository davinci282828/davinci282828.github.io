COVERAGE OVERVIEW

Does TRICARE Cover Mental Health?

Yes. Tricare covers outpatient mental health services, including individual psychotherapy, when medically necessary. Coverage applies to all Tricare-eligible beneficiaries — active-duty service members, retirees, and dependents enrolled in Tricare Select or Tricare Reserve Select.

When you see an in-network Tricare therapist, sessions are covered subject to your plan's copay and deductible requirements. Our Therapy Coordinator verifies your specific coverage before your first appointment.

✓
Conditions Covered by TRICARE — anxiety, depression, PTSD, military sexual trauma, deployment stress
✓
Active Duty Family (Group A) — typically $0 copay for in-network behavioral health
✓
Retirees & Dependents (Group B) — approximately 25% cost-share after deductible
✓
Telehealth = In-Person Rates — covered at the same rate as face-to-face visits
✓
Reserve Select (TRS) — 20% cost-share after $150/$300 deductible
✓
Catastrophic Cap — annual out-of-pocket maximum protects against high costs
