COVERAGE OVERVIEW

Does TRICARE Cover Mental Health?

Yes. Tricare covers outpatient mental health services, including individual psychotherapy, when medically necessary. Coverage applies to active-duty service members, retirees, and dependents enrolled in Tricare Select or Tricare Reserve Select.

✓
Conditions Covered — anxiety, depression, PTSD, MST, deployment stress
✓
Active Duty (Group A) — typically $0 copay in-network
✓
Retirees (Group B) — ~25% cost-share after deductible
✓
Telehealth — same rate as in-person visits
